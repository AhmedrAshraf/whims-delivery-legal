Delete your Whims Delivery account

Whims Delivery is operated by Whims Delivery Service, Abu Dhabi, UAE. You can delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open the Whims Delivery app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm by tapping Delete.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email

Email admin@whims.ae from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". Include the phone number on your account. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 7 days. Drivers and staff whose accounts were created by Whims should use this option.

What gets deleted

Deleted: your login, name, email, phone and WhatsApp numbers, saved addresses, profile photo and notifications.

Kept: records of completed orders and payments (amounts, dates and delivery addresses), which we must keep for accounting, tax and legal purposes for up to 5 years. They are not linked to an active account and are not used for any other purpose.

See our Privacy Policy for more details.

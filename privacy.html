Whims Delivery

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

This policy explains how Whims Delivery Service ("Whims", "we", "us"), a same-day delivery service based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, collects, uses and protects information when you use the Whims Delivery mobile app and related services (whims-delivery.com). The app is used by our customers (individuals and businesses), our delivery drivers, and our staff.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, company name (for business customers), profile photo, and a password (stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider).
  • Order information: pickup and delivery addresses and their map coordinates, recipient name and phone number, pickup and delivery times, order notes, delivery fees, and order history.
  • Payment information: payment method, amounts, payment status, cash collected and credit balances. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we never receive or store your full card number.
  • Location (drivers): if you use the app as a driver, we collect your device's precise location while the app is open so coordinators and customers can track active deliveries. Customers and staff may also share a location to set pickup or delivery points.
  • Photos: photos you take or choose, such as proof-of-delivery photos, expense receipts and profile pictures.
  • Staff and driver records: for our own employees and drivers, work information such as vehicle details, deliveries completed, fines, expenses and payroll.
  • Notifications: in-app messages we send you about deliveries and payments.

We do not use advertising SDKs, we do not sell your information, and we do not use your data for advertising.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To receive, schedule, dispatch and deliver orders, and to show live delivery tracking.
  • To process payments, issue receipts and statements, and reconcile cash collected on your behalf.
  • To contact you about your orders, payments and account (in-app, by phone, email or WhatsApp).
  • To run our business, including driver management, payroll and preventing fraud or misuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations in the UAE.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to provide the service:

  • Within a delivery: the assigned driver sees pickup and delivery details. Customers can see their driver's name and live location during an active delivery. Anyone you send a tracking link to can see that order's status and driver location.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and server functions), Stripe (card payments), and OpenStreetMap services (map tiles, address search and routing; your location or addresses are sent to them to display maps and routes).
  • Legal: when required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Sign-in sessions are stored in your device's secure storage, and access to data is limited by role (customer, driver, staff).

Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your personal information, except order and payment records we must keep for accounting, tax or legal purposes (normally up to 5 years). Those records are retained in a restricted form and are not used for any other purpose.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and update your profile in the app.
  • You can deny or revoke location, camera and photo permissions in your device settings. Some features (such as driver tracking or proof of delivery) may not work without them.
  • You can delete your account at any time. See how to delete your account.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing admin@whims.ae.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and change the effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
